# Request

Replace the Aha section's content ([`docs/index.html`](../../docs/index.html)) with a new copy set:

> **The best engineering now happens in conversation. It shouldn't disappear with it.**
>
> Working with agentic AI creates far more than code. Every conversation with your agentic AI produces decisions, trade-offs, conventions, and future ideas that shape your project.
>
> AGENTIC_BOOTSTRAP makes them a lasting part of your repository.
>
> > Opinionated enough to get started. Flexible enough to become your own.
>
> Start with the bootstrap. Then make it yours. Add your own skills, workflows, standards, templates, or conventions—or remove what doesn't fit. Your operating model grows with your engineering.

The user also shared a meta-observation: the opening two-sentence slogan (*"The best engineering now happens in conversation. It shouldn't disappear with it."*) has the same shape as *Infrastructure as Code* / *Build once. Run anywhere.* — it describes a category shift in software engineering, not the product. Preserve it verbatim through future iterations.

## Reasoning

The previous Aha (chip-row shape: eyebrow chip + four knowledge-type chips + *Things worth keeping / Most of it disappears / AGENTIC_BOOTSTRAP keeps it* triplet) delivered R2 as a minimal beat but its emphasis fell on the *disappears* problem framing rather than the *category shift* naming. The new copy leads with the category shift — *the best engineering now happens in conversation* — and lets the substrate come in as a matter of fact (*"makes them a lasting part of your repository"*), rather than as the punchline of a *disappears → keeps* contrast.

The new blockquote (*"Opinionated enough to get started. Flexible enough to become your own."*) and the closing extension paragraph (*"Start with the bootstrap. Then make it yours..."*) do work the previous shape didn't: they answer the implicit *"is this yet another opinionated framework that constrains me?"* objection before the demo section sets expectations for the mechanism.

Section grows from ~50 words to ~110 words. Still short, but no longer *tiny* in the way the previous chip-row was. The `py-16` → `py-20` bump gives the taller content breathing room without competing with the demo's `py-24`. Container narrowed from `max-w-3xl` (centered chip-row wanted horizontal spread) to `max-w-2xl` (prose reads better at narrower widths); text alignment shifts from centered to left-aligned for the same reason.
