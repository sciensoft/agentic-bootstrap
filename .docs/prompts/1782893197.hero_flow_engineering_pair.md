# Request

Roll back `docs/index.html` to a pre-restructure state (already done manually by the user) and replace the hero copy with a new three-beat structure:

- **Eyebrow:** *Agentic AI changed how we build software.*
- **H1:** *Keep the flow. Keep the engineering.*
- **Subhead:** *The fastest engineering conversations now happen between humans and AI. AGENTIC_BOOTSTRAP makes sure the architecture, decisions, trade-offs, and future ideas survive long after the session ends.*

## Reasoning

The [ADR-0010](../adrs/0010-reposition-as-engineering-operating-model.md) R1 hero — a 5-line category-shift staircase (*Software / engineering / changed.*) resolving into a 2-line slogan pair (*The day-one discipline. / At Agentic AI speed.*) — didn't land on a product read-through. The staircase overwhelmed the resolution and the first read felt like slogan-piled-on-slogan rather than framing-then-resolution. The category-shift thesis (agentic AI produced abundance of engineering knowledge; the substrate makes it durable) is not in question; only the hero copy shape is.

The new hero pulls the category-shift assertion out of the H1 and into the eyebrow (where it reads as declarative context, not dramatic pacing), collapses the H1 to a two-line imperative directive pair (*Keep the flow. / Keep the engineering.*), and merges the ADR-0010-original lede and subhead into a single two-sentence subhead that carries the *fastest conversations → survive the session* thesis.

Structurally the hero collapses from four beats (eyebrow + H1 + lede + subhead) to three (eyebrow + H1 + subhead). The right-column terminal codeblock and the auxiliary hero chrome (four checkmarks, Works-with strip) stay under the reasoning already documented in ADR-0010's Consequences.

The revised copy is checked against ADR-0010's *Protagonist convention*: the eyebrow's subject is *Agentic AI* (a producer of engineering knowledge — inside the protagonist set); the H1's subject is the implicit *you* (imperative verb form); the subhead's subjects are *the fastest engineering conversations* (the collaboration) and *AGENTIC_BOOTSTRAP* (the product). The repository does not appear as the subject of an active verb anywhere in the main beats.
