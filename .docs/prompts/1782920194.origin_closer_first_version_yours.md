# Request

Replace the origin section's closing positioning line ([`docs/index.html`](../../docs/index.html)):

- **From:** *"AGENTIC_BOOTSTRAP isn't the operating model. It's the starting point for yours."*
- **To:** *"AGENTIC_BOOTSTRAP gives you the first version of your operating model. What it becomes is yours."*

## Reasoning

The previous closer was structurally a *negation-then-resolution* (*isn't ... it's ...*) — reads slightly defensive, like a comparison against the accusation *"this is trying to be a fixed operating model."* The new closer is affirmative-then-personal — it plants a factual claim (*"gives you the first version"*) and then hands ownership over (*"What it becomes is yours"*). Reads more like a gift than a disclaimer, and lines up better with the section's overall arc: the maker built something, they hand it to you, and what comes next is your call.

The pattern also creates a nice callback to the action stanza just above (*Start with the bootstrap. Keep what works. Remove what doesn't. Add your own... Evolve it with your project.*) — the stanza describes the *how*, the closer names the *what* (a first version → your version).
