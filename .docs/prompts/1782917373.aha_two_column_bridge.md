# Request

The Aha section still isn't bridging the hero and 60-second demo nicely. Widen the content area and restructure into a two-column layout: **left** carries the H2 tagline + the "conversation produces engineering knowledge" body paragraph; **right** carries the AGENTIC_BOOTSTRAP product hook + the "Opinionated enough to get started. Flexible enough to become your own." blockquote; **full width bottom** carries the "Start with the bootstrap. Then make it yours..." extension paragraph. All content retained; the shrink is layout-only.

## Reasoning

The prior visual-hierarchy compression ([prompt 1782916530](./1782916530.aha_bridge_visual_hierarchy.md)) shrank the type tiers and section padding but kept the narrow `max-w-2xl` single-column stack. That produced a shelf between two wide `max-w-7xl` sections (hero above, demo below) — the narrow column read as a discontinuity, not a bridge. The user's diagnosis is right: horizontal continuity with the surrounding sections is what makes a bridge feel like one.

Splitting into columns also lets the *problem statement* (left) and the *solution statement* (right) sit side-by-side rather than stacked, which reads as *"here's what's at stake ↔ here's what the substrate does about it"* — a horizontal contrast the eye traverses in the time it takes to move down from hero to demo. The extension paragraph stays full-width below because it's a follow-up beat (*"and here's how you extend it"*) that would feel cramped in either column.

Container: section wrapper at `max-w-7xl mx-auto px-6` to match hero/demo, then the inner content constrained to `max-w-5xl mx-auto` so the two columns stay in reading widths (~450px each after `gap-16`). This gives the *bridge* section the same horizontal presence as the sections around it without over-widening the actual copy.

## Layout math

- `max-w-7xl` = 80rem = 1280px (outer section container)
- `max-w-5xl` = 64rem = 1024px (inner content container)
- `md:gap-16` = 4rem = 64px (inter-column gap)
- Column width at desktop ≈ (1024 − 64) / 2 = **480px** each — comfortable reading width, similar to a Medium article column.
- `max-w-3xl` = 48rem = 768px (extension paragraph cap) — reads at book-comfortable width.
