# Request

Refine the hero H1 from the parallel-verb pair *Keep the flow. Keep the engineering.* (landed in the previous commit under ADR-0010's *R1 revised*) to a single directive with a compound object: *Keep the flow, and the engineering.*

## Reasoning

The parallel-verb pair read slightly slogan-y — the second *Keep* creates a *verse-chorus* cadence that lands like advertising copy. Collapsing to a single *Keep* with a comma-and construction gives the sentence a spoken cadence: the reader completes the thought after *flow*, pauses on the comma, then lands the second object. Reads more conversational and more inviting; more like advice a colleague would give than a slogan a landing page recites.

The two nouns (*flow*, *engineering*) and their meanings are unchanged — *flow* still names the natural affordance of human–AI collaboration, *engineering* still names the discipline that has to survive it. Gradient still highlights *engineering* alone.

## Output

- **[`docs/index.html`](../../docs/index.html)** — H1 spans updated. The two visual `<span class="block">` blocks are retained but their content is now *"Keep the flow,"* and *"and the engineering."* — a force-broken rendering of one sentence, not two sentences. V1 sizing (`text-5xl md:text-6xl lg:text-7xl`) is kept because the sentence at that tier is too long for a single line inside `lg:col-span-7`; breaking at the comma preserves the one-sentence rhythm without shrinking the type.
- **[`.docs/adrs/0010-reposition-as-engineering-operating-model.md`](../adrs/0010-reposition-as-engineering-operating-model.md)** — the *R1 revised* H1 spec paragraph is updated in place. The descriptor changes from *"two lines"* to *"single sentence rendered as two visual blocks"*, the rationale changes from *imperative parallel pair* to *imperative directive with a compound object*, and a note documents the same-day iteration from *Keep the flow. Keep the engineering.* → *Keep the flow, and the engineering.*
