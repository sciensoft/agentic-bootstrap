# 10. Reposition as engineering operating model for human–AI software development; supersede ADR-0009

- **Status**: Accepted (supersedes [ADR-0009](./0009-reposition-as-engineering-memory-system-with-ab-test.md))
- **Date**: 2026-06-30

## Context

[ADR-0009](./0009-reposition-as-engineering-memory-system-with-ab-test.md) repositioned the project as an *engineering-memory system* and A/B/C-tested the new framing on the landing-page hero. That repositioning was a step forward — it moved every public surface away from mechanism-first ("one markdown file, any agent, one prompt") toward consequence ("AI sessions end; engineering knowledge shouldn't"). The hero pinned to variant B while baseline data accumulated; the README's lede and "What lands" table pivoted in parallel.

A subsequent product-strategy review found the positioning still describes the **consequence** rather than the **underlying problem**. *Repositories should remember engineering* is true but inert — repositories have always failed to remember engineering, and that fact alone doesn't explain why **now** is the moment to fix it. The actual change in the world is this:

> Agentic AI has fundamentally increased the amount of engineering knowledge produced during a software-engineering session.

A single afternoon of human–AI collaboration now generates more architectural alternatives, trade-off discussions, rejected approaches, future ideas, naming decisions, security considerations, testing ideas, and implementation rationale than a typical week of solo work. The bottleneck used to be *creating* engineering knowledge; the bottleneck is now *retaining* it. This is a category-creation argument — parallel to Infrastructure-as-Code emerging when cloud became programmable, GitOps emerging when Kubernetes reshaped infrastructure management, containerization emerging when deployment shapes changed. AGENTIC_BOOTSTRAP exists because agentic AI changed software engineering; the substrate it installs is what makes the resulting engineering knowledge durable.

The implications: ADR-0009's hero copy, the structural plan tracked in [`landing-positioning-restructure.md`](../todos/landing-positioning-restructure.md), and the "five-memory-types ↔ artifacts" framing all need to be revised — they still lead with *what the bootstrap produces* (a memory substrate) rather than *what just changed in software engineering, and why repositories now need this*. The new positioning supersedes ADR-0009; the A/B/C experiment infrastructure that ADR-0009 introduced survives intact, but only one variant ships now (the new curiosity-led copy) with the engine wired for future tests.

## Decision

**Adopt as the project's primary noun phrase:** *an engineering operating model for human–AI software development*. Lead every public surface with the **category shift** — agentic AI changed how much engineering knowledge a session produces — before introducing the project name, the substrate, or any artifact. The substrate (`AGENTS.md`, `.agents/rules/`, `.docs/adrs/`, `.docs/prompts/`, `.docs/todos/`, `.docs/security/methodology.md`) is reframed as *evidence of the philosophy*, not the philosophy itself.

**Tagline:** *"Agentic AI has made engineering knowledge abundant. AGENTIC_BOOTSTRAP makes it durable."*

**Landing-page hero** ([`docs/index.html`](../../docs/index.html)) — one variant. **Iterated on 2026-07-01** ([prompt file](../prompts/1782893197.hero_flow_engineering_pair.md)): the copy specified for R1 in the *R1 initial* subsection below was rolled back after a product read-through — the 5-line staircase overwhelmed the resolution pair and made the first read feel like slogan-piled-on-slogan rather than framing-then-resolution. The thesis (agentic-AI category shift → durability of engineering knowledge) is unchanged; the copy is reshaped around a shorter directive pair.

### R1 revised (current, 2026-07-01)

- **Eyebrow:** *Agentic AI changed how we build software.* — the category-shift assertion moves out of the H1 staircase and into the eyebrow, where it reads as declarative context rather than dramatic pacing. Short enough for the pill-badge chip; the full-sentence framing (no `·` separators) signals *this is the shift the rest of the page explains*, not a taxonomy tag.
- **H1:** single sentence rendered as two visual blocks, `text-5xl md:text-6xl lg:text-7xl font-extrabold tracking-tight leading-[1.05]` (v1 sizing — the smaller `text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl` tier from the *R1 initial* draft was needed for the 5-line staircase but reads too small for a hero of this weight; the sentence in turn is too long to fit a single line at v1 tier within `lg:col-span-7`, so it is force-broken at the comma so both blocks read together as one sentence):
  ```
  Keep the flow,
  and the engineering.
  ```
  Imperative directive with a compound object — the reader is the implicit subject of a single *Keep*, honouring the *Protagonist convention*, and the comma-and construction gives the sentence a spoken cadence (the pause after *flow* is where the reader completes the thought before the second object lands). *Flow* names the natural affordance of human–AI collaboration (the pace, the conversational tempo, the fact that engineering happens as talk); *engineering* names the discipline that has to survive it (decisions, architecture, trade-offs, retained rationale). Same-day iteration from an earlier *Keep the flow. Keep the engineering.* draft: the repeated-verb parallel pair read slightly slogan-y (*verse-chorus*) where a single directive with a comma-and reads more conversational and more inviting. Gradient is applied to *engineering* alone (the differentiator noun), echoing v1's underline-on-*Agent* pattern of spotlighting a single identity word without decorating the whole line.
- **Subhead:** *The fastest engineering conversations now happen between engineers and agentic AI. AGENTIC_BOOTSTRAP continuously turns their architecture, decisions, trade-offs, and future ideas into lasting project knowledge—without breaking the flow.* — the *R1 initial* lede and long-form subhead collapse into a single two-sentence paragraph. First sentence names what changed (the collaboration itself is the fastest engineering conversation — the category shift humanised, without saying *"faster than solo work"* which reads as marketing). Second sentence names what the product does about it: the four categories of engineering knowledge most at risk from session-boundedness — *architecture, decisions, trade-offs, future ideas* — map onto ADRs, prompt files, ADR-consequences sections, and `.docs/todos/` respectively, though no file names appear on the hero. *Idempotent · cross-stack · tool-agnostic* and the *day-one discipline* file-list vignette both drop out of the hero at this iteration; they migrate downstream — *tool-agnostic* to R6's Works-with band, *day-one discipline* to R5's substrate framing — where the concrete anchoring lands with more room. Same-day iteration against an earlier *"humans and AI"* / *"makes sure ... survive long after the session ends"* draft tightens four things: *humans* → *engineers* pins the professional identity of the person in the collaboration; *AI* → *agentic AI* echoes the eyebrow's *Agentic AI changed how we build software* and rules out chat-window Copilot-style tools that aren't the referent; *makes sure ... survive* → *continuously turns ... into lasting project knowledge* swaps a passive-guardian framing for an active present-continuous one and names the output as a category engineers already have a mental model for; and the closing beat *—without breaking the flow* (em-dash unspaced for a snap cadence) mirrors the H1's *Keep the flow, and the engineering.*, landing the reader on the H1's promise made concrete — the discipline that has to survive doesn't tax the tempo. Emphasis weights: `text-zinc-100 font-medium` on *the fastest engineering conversations now happen between engineers and agentic AI* (primary framing); `text-gradient font-semibold` on *AGENTIC_BOOTSTRAP* (product-name visual anchor); `text-zinc-100 font-medium` on *without breaking the flow* (H1 callback; replaces the earlier *survive long after the session ends* emphasis while carrying the same *abundant / durable* contrast weight from the withdrawn *R1 initial* lede).

The **Lede** slot from *R1 initial* is retired — its *abundant → durable* contrast is absorbed into the revised subhead's two-sentence structure, so the hero collapses from four beats (eyebrow + H1 + lede + subhead) to three (eyebrow + H1 + subhead). The right-column terminal codeblock stays under the same reasoning documented in Consequences.

### R1 initial (superseded 2026-07-01, kept for rationale)

- **Eyebrow:** *An engineering memory system for AI-coded repos* — chosen for grasp-at-a-glance simplicity over the more abstract *"operating model for human–AI software development"* phrasing (the latter survives as the project's positioning noun in the README and ADRs, just not on the landing surface where first-read clarity matters more).
- **H1:** five lines, `text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl font-extrabold tracking-tight leading-[1.05]`:
  ```
  Software            ← staircase (single word per line)
  engineering         ←
  changed.            ←
  The day-one discipline.   ← gradient
  At Agentic AI speed.      ← gradient
  ```
  Lines 1–3 staircase the category-shift assertion in dramatic single-word pacing. Lines 4–5 (both in `text-gradient`) name what the bootstrap delivers: the **day-one discipline** (the rigor a Senior Engineer would have set up on day one — anchoring the reader in something familiar; substance carried by the subhead) at **Agentic AI speed** (the new delivery dimension — automated, applied at every commit, matching the pace AI now produces work). The two halves of the resolution pair form an *adjective-noun ↔ prepositional-phrase* parallel that reads as a slogan. H1 type tier sits one step below the standard (`text-7xl` was crowding the longer line-4 sentence past the `lg:col-span-7` container at desktop); the smaller tier lets both resolution lines fit on single lines at every breakpoint without wrapping.

  Four prior drafts were withdrawn during iteration: *"Repositories haven't"* (put the *repository* in the protagonist role; see *Protagonist convention*); *"You're losing most of it"* (correct on protagonist but framed line 4 as the gap rather than the resolution); *"So does the discipline"* (positive and parallel-rhythm correct, but implied the discipline itself changed — it didn't); *"The discipline runs at AI speed"* (single-line variant of the chosen pair; user preferred the two-line *day-one discipline + at Agentic AI speed* slogan structure that echoes the v1 H1's *day-one discipline* line while incorporating the new speed dimension). A fifth withdrawal follows on 2026-07-01: the pair itself was rolled back because the staircase overwhelmed the resolution — see *R1 revised* above.
- **Lede:** *Agentic AI makes engineering knowledge **abundant**. AGENTIC_BOOTSTRAP makes it **durable**.* — the brief's tagline, with one tightening: *"has made"* → *"makes"* so both sentences share the present-tense parallel verb *makes*, creating a tighter contrast between the two halves of the pair. `font-medium` on the *abundant / durable* contrast pair. **Absorbed into the R1 revised subhead** (the *abundant → durable* contrast survives as *the fastest engineering conversations … survive long after the session ends*).
- **Subhead:** *One markdown file. Any agentic coding assistant. One prompt. Your repo grows the day-one discipline a Senior Engineer would have set up — workflow rules, ADR culture, the testing pyramid, telemetry, security rubric, TODO discipline — then bundles every change into one focused git commit. Tool-agnostic. Cross-stack. Idempotent.* — substantively the v1 subhead, with *"day-one"* pulled forward as an adjective so the central clause reads *"the day-one discipline a Senior Engineer would have set up"* (mirrors H1 line 4). Three earlier drafts were withdrawn (engineering-memory swap, retention reframe, walks-in-with-all-of-it reframe) because they over-corrected on the brief's *"never lead with file names"* guidance — *that* rule applies to the **main hero beats** (eyebrow, H1, lede), not to supporting copy where a concrete file-shaped list anchors the abstract discipline in things the reader recognizes. *Idempotent* is restored (the prior turn had swapped it to *Re-runnable*); both words mean the same thing but *Idempotent* is the v1 wording and is a term any engineer reads cleanly. **Retired at R1 revised** — the file-shaped vignette migrates to R5, the *tool-agnostic · cross-stack · idempotent* trilogy migrates to R6.

The hero is **hybrid** by design: the eyebrow + H1 + lede create the framing, and the subhead + terminal codeblock supply enough concrete identity (one file, runs through your agent, scaffold lands) that the visitor doesn't bounce on philosophy alone. This is a softening of the brief's stricter "create curiosity only, no mechanism" guidance — kept as a deliberate trade-off because the project's *bootstrap-ness* is identity-level, not mechanism-level, and stripping it strips what the project IS.

**A/B/C engine.** The variant-selection infrastructure from ADR-0009 (synchronous `<head>` IIFE; `localStorage` stickiness; bot pinning; `gtag` `experiment_view` event; CSS attribute selectors hiding non-matching `data-hero-variant-block` elements; `FORCE_VARIANT` override) is preserved end-to-end. Adding a future variant B (or D, etc.) is a localized change: add a sibling `data-hero-variant-block` block in `<body>`, restore the weighted roll in `pickWeighted()`, set `FORCE_VARIANT = null`.

## Consequences

- **Supersedes ADR-0009.** ADR-0009's *engineering-memory system* framing was a step toward this position but stopped short of naming the category shift. Its status line is updated to `Superseded by ADR-0010`; the body is preserved for history (the A/B/C experiment infrastructure it documents remains active under this ADR).
- **Stale TODO replaced, not edited.** [`landing-positioning-restructure.md`](../todos/landing-positioning-restructure.md) is removed (`git rm` in this commit) and replaced by [`landing-restart-operating-model.md`](../todos/landing-restart-operating-model.md). Its 8-phase plan and "5 memory types ↔ artifacts" framing don't survive the reframe.
- **A/B engine kept; variant set collapsed.** Future tests cost a few lines (add a new `data-hero-variant-block` block + restore weights in `pickWeighted` + set `FORCE_VARIANT = null`). No infrastructure was deleted — only the B and C variant blocks in `<body>`. `experiment_view` continues to fire with `variant: "A"` and `experiment_id: "hero_2026_q3"`; baseline-conversion data from the pre-supersede pinned-to-B window remains in GA4 and can be retroactively segmented by `experiment_view` time-range.
- **Right-column terminal codeblock kept.** The terminal-styled visual (showing the bootstrap interview Q&A and the resulting scaffold file tree) stays beside the hero copy in a 7/5 grid split. An earlier draft of this ADR proposed removing it on a strict reading of the brief's *"no implementation in the hero"* guidance; user feedback ([prompt 1782852148](../prompts/1782852148.hero_simplify_eyebrow_h1_subhead_terminal.md)) clarified that the terminal visual is identity-level (it's what *bootstrap* looks like), not mechanism-level, and that removing it amputates a core affordance — *this is a thing you can run, and here is what running it looks like in five lines*. The visual stays; replacement with a different illustration would itself be an ADR-worthy decision.
- **Protagonist convention** (established under this ADR via user feedback; applies to all future hero/body copy decisions). The actors in this product's story are: *human–AI collaboration* (the producer of engineering knowledge — the brief's *single cognitive system*), *the engineering knowledge itself* (the thing produced and preserved), *AGENTIC_BOOTSTRAP* (the operating model that makes the knowledge durable), and *the discipline* (the bootstrap-installed rigor by which the repo retains knowledge). The repository is the *setting/canvas* where the knowledge lands — never the *painter*. In **main hero beats** (eyebrow, H1, lede) the repository must not appear as the subject of an active verb. In **supporting copy** (subhead, body sections) the repository may appear as a *possessed subject* (*"your repo grows the discipline"*, *"your repo carries the memory"*) because the reader reads possessed subjects as instruments they own, not as competing protagonists. Two H1 drafts violated the main-beat half of this rule and were withdrawn: *"Repositories haven't"* (repo as the failed actor); the convention is now codified to prevent the recurrence. Future positive-resolution copy should default to *the discipline*, *the engineering*, *the collaboration*, or the implicit *you* as the subject of any active verb in the H1 or lede.
- **Auxiliary hero chrome stays transitionally.** The four checkmarks (Project-agnostic · Re-runnable · No dependencies · One file) and the eight-tool *Works with* strip are not philosophy and don't fit the new hero spine, but deleting them in R1 would leave a product-information gap until R6 re-stages them in dedicated bands below the philosophical sections. They stay as transitional chrome and migrate cleanly in R6.
- **CHANGELOG not bumped.** This change does not touch `AGENTIC-BOOTSTRAP.md`'s header version. Landing-page positioning lives outside the artifact lint contract.
- **OG / meta tags not changed in R1.** They currently encode the variant-B copy from ADR-0009. They will be updated in R2 or R3 once the body text settles around the new framing — changing them in R1 alone would mismatch the live page during the in-flight restructure. Crawlers therefore still see *"AI sessions end. Engineering knowledge shouldn't."* until the meta sweep lands; acceptable as a short transient.
- **The substrate's substance doesn't change.** ADRs, prompts, TODOs, security rubric, rules, best-practices, the bootstrap artifact itself — none of these files or their contents shift. Only the *story we tell about them* shifts. The bootstrap remains a single-file, self-contained, idempotent, tool-agnostic operator playbook.
- **Runtime footprint kept.** The existing *Runtime footprint* section (~25k smart-agent scaffold / ~80k naive-fallback scaffold / ~10k every session after) is structurally important under the new positioning — it answers the implicit *"won't this engineering-memory substrate cost a fortune in tokens?"* objection that the new framing surfaces. Section stays; its copy gets a light reframe in R6 to lead with *durability without per-session tax* rather than *heavyweight scaffold / lightweight runtime*.
- **Stronger commitments downstream.** Any future ADR that adds a substrate artifact, a rule, or a tool adapter should reason from *"does this strengthen the durability of the engineering knowledge a human–AI session produces?"* — not from *"does this add a feature?"* The framing is load-bearing.
